## What we do

Most padel clubs take bookings through a marketplace like Playtomic. It sends them players, which genuinely helps. But the player signs up with the marketplace, not the club. So the club gets the booking and the marketplace gets the customer details.

Deuce is the other option. Members book on the club's own app, and the club keeps the list of who they are.

## Who we sell to

**Primary buyer**
- Title: Owner. Often owner-operator, often on court themselves three times a week.
- Company type: Independent padel clubs, 1 to 3 locations, mostly founded since 2021
- Size: 4 to 12 courts, €200k to €900k revenue
- Geography: Flanders, Netherlands

**Secondary**
- The club manager. Doesn't sign, but lives in the system all day and will make an owner's life miserable about a bad one.

**Core pain**
- Tuesday at 2pm the club is empty. Thursday at 7pm three people want the same court.
- People book and don't turn up. That costs about 11% of what the club could have earned. Most owners don't chase it because it feels rude.
- Coaching, leagues and social play are each planned somewhere different. Usually a spreadsheet, sometimes a WhatsApp group with 84 people in it.
- Owners who use a marketplace often don't realise until later that they can't contact their own regulars.

**Anti-ICP: who we don't sell to**
- **One or two courts.** A phone and a wall grid genuinely works at that size. We chased eleven of these in Q1, closed four, and three churned inside five months. Stop.
- **Chains over 8 locations.** They want multi-site reporting and API access we don't have yet. We'd win the pilot and lose the rollout.
- **Tennis clubs.** Similar on the surface, different underneath: membership-driven rather than booking-driven, older members, slower to move. Different product.
- **Clubs that get most of their bookings from the marketplace.** If 60% of their business comes from Playtomic players who aren't members, leaving would cost them more than they'd save. We should tell them so.

## How we're positioned

Your club, your members, your data.

Marketplaces fix a real problem: empty courts. They fix it by sitting between the club and the player. That's a good trade for a new club with nobody in it. It's a worse trade once the club is busy.

**What we're really up against:** for small clubs, a phone and a paper grid on the wall. For everyone else, Playtomic. Playtomic is the real fight.

## Key differentiators

- **You own the member.** Your branding, your email list, your export button. Proof: full CSV export of everything, on any plan, no support ticket needed.
- **Built by someone who ran a club.** Sofie ran six courts near Antwerp for four years. Dynamic pricing exists because Tuesday afternoons nearly killed her. Proof: she does most of the sales calls.
- **Flat fee, no commission.** €149/month regardless of volume. Marketplaces take 8 to 15% of bookings they source. Proof: our pricing page has one number on it.

## GTM motion

- **Motion:** Sales-led, founder-led at this stage
- **Path to customer:** club owner WhatsApp groups and word of mouth → website → 20-minute call with Sofie → 30-day trial → close
- **Sales cycle:** 2 to 5 weeks. Notably faster after a bad month or a commission increase.
- **Decision:** Owner decides, usually alone, often on a Sunday evening.

## Rules & guardrails

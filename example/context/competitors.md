## Profiles

### Playtomic

| | |
|---|---|
| **Website** | playtomic.io |
| **What they do** | Marketplace and booking platform. Players find courts, clubs get filled. |
| **Target customer** | Both sides. Players first, clubs as supply. |
| **Lead message** | Find and book padel anywhere |
| **Pricing** | Commission on sourced bookings, 8 to 15%. Raised to 15% in Spain, May 2026. |
| **Strengths** | Huge number of players. It really does fill empty courts. Good app. For a brand new club it's hard to beat. |
| **Weaknesses** | The player's account is with Playtomic, not the club. The club gets no email list and no way to export the data. The more the club grows, the more commission it pays. |
| **How we win** | Not on features. On what the club gives up. Ask an owner whether their regulars could name the club or the app. Works on clubs over 18 months old. Doesn't work on new empty clubs, and we should say so. |

*Source: playtomic.io pricing and club pages, Spanish commission change confirmed via two customer conversations, 2026-05*

### Matchi

| | |
|---|---|
| **Website** | matchi.se |
| **What they do** | Booking platform for racket sports, strong in Scandinavia |
| **Target customer** | Tennis and padel clubs, mid-size and up |
| **Lead message** | Booking platform for racket sports |
| **Pricing** | Subscription plus transaction fee |
| **Strengths** | Mature product, multi-sport, established in Nordics |
| **Weaknesses** | Built for tennis first. Padel formats like americanos and fixed doubles were added later and it shows. Barely present in Benelux. |

*Source: matchi.se, accessed 2026-04*

### Phone + wall grid

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A laminated week grid and Marc's mobile number |
| **Target customer** | Every club, by default, until it hurts |

## White space

- **Nobody talks about who owns the customer.** Everyone sells convenience and more players. Nobody says out loud what a club gives up. It's an awkward subject, which is why it's still available.
- **Nobody takes empty hours seriously.** Tuesday at 2pm is the real problem in this business and everyone treats it as a footnote.
- **Nobody talks to owners as business owners.** The whole market treats clubs as venues to be managed. Owners see themselves as running a small business with a community attached.

## What everyone says (and therefore means nothing)

- "Grow your community"
- "The future of padel"

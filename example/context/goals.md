# Goals & KPIs

## This period's focus

> Get to 120 paying clubs before the indoor season starts in autumn. And stop relying entirely on Sofie already knowing people. By the end of September, half of new business should come from people who found us themselves.

## KPIs & targets

| Metric | Baseline | Target | Source | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paying clubs | 58 | 120 | Stripe | weekly |
| Inbound demo requests | 9/mo | 30/mo | HubSpot | weekly |
| Inbound share of pipeline | 18% | 50% | HubSpot | monthly |
| Trial to paid conversion | 44% | 55% | product analytics | monthly |
| Off-peak utilisation lift in customer clubs | +6pts | +12pts | product analytics | monthly |

*The last one is about our customers, not our marketing, and it's here on purpose. If clubs fill more of their quiet hours, they stay, they tell other owners, and we get a case study. One number, three jobs.*

## What we're deliberately not chasing

- **Getting known by players.** We don't sell to players and we're not building a consumer brand. Money spent reaching them helps a marketplace, not us.
- **Spain.** Not until 2027, and not without someone local. Spanish enquiries get an honest "not yet".
- **Clubs with one or two courts.** See the anti-ICP. We lost money on eleven of them in Q1.
- **Follower counts.** LinkedIn is here to reach about 600 club owners in Benelux. That's the only thing it's for.

## Constraints

- **Budget:** €3.5k a month. About half goes on events and club visits, which bring in more customers than anything we've tried online.
- **People:** me, a part-time videographer, Sofie for calls and content
- **Time:** the indoor season starts late September. Clubs buy in August or they wait until January.

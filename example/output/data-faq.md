# Output: data FAQ answer

**The prompt, both times:** *"Write the FAQ answer for Deuce to the question: what happens to my member data if I leave?"*

The only difference is that the second one had the context layer to read.

---

# Without the context

**What happens to my data if I leave?**

We're sorry to see you go! At Deuce, we believe your data belongs to you. If you decide to leave, our support team will be happy to help you export your information and ensure a smooth transition. Just reach out and we'll take care of the rest.

---

# With the context

**What happens to my member data if I leave?**

You take all of it with you. On any plan, you can export your members, their contact details, your bookings and your history as a CSV file. You do it yourself from your account. No support ticket, no waiting, no fee.

This is the reason Deuce exists. If your bookings ran through a marketplace, leaving would mean starting your member list from nothing, because those players have an account with Playtomic, not with you. Here, the list was always yours.

---

# What changed, and why

The first answer is what any software company says. The second one turns a support question into the argument for the whole product.

| The first version says | The context says | Where |
|---|---|---|
| "We're sorry to see you go!" with an exclamation mark | Warm and direct, no exclamation marks. Clear first, funny second. | `brand-guidelines.md` → voice |
| "our support team will be happy to help you export" | You do it yourself. Full CSV export on any plan, no support ticket. | `business-context.md` → differentiator 1 |
| "ensure a smooth transition" | Say the specifics, not "smooth". Numbers and facts, not adjectives. | `brand-guidelines.md` → how we write |
| Never says why any of this matters | This is the whole point of Deuce. Set it against the marketplace. | `business-context.md` → how we're positioned |
| Could be any SaaS answer | Name Playtomic. Owners know who we mean. | `competitors.md` → Playtomic |

The line that matters is the last one. A generic answer treats leaving as a support task. The real answer uses the question to say what the club owns with Deuce and would not own with a marketplace. That framing lives in the context, not in the prompt.

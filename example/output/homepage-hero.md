# Output: homepage hero

**The prompt, both times:** *"Write the homepage hero section for Deuce, booking software for padel clubs. Give me a headline, a subhead, and a button."*

The only difference is that the second one had the context layer to read.

---

# Without the context

**Headline:** The complete platform for modern padel clubs

**Subhead:** Streamline your bookings, grow your community, and unlock the full potential of every court. Everything your club needs, all in one place.

**Button:** Start your free trial

---

# With the context

**Headline:** Your members book with you. Not with a marketplace.

**Subhead:** Deuce is booking software for padel clubs that want to keep their own member list. Your app, your branding, your data. €149 a month, no commission.

**Button:** Book a 20-minute call with Sofie

---

# What changed, and why

The first hero could sit on top of any booking product for any kind of venue. Swap padel for yoga and nothing breaks. That is the test it fails.

| The first version says | The context says | Where |
|---|---|---|
| "The complete platform for modern padel clubs" | Banned. If Playtomic could put it on their homepage, it isn't ours. | `brand-guidelines.md` → what we avoid |
| "Streamline", "grow your community", "unlock the full potential" | All banned. Don't use "community" as if it's a number. | `brand-guidelines.md` → especially avoid |
| Leads with the platform | Lead with the club, not the software. Say "we", not "the platform". | `business-context.md` → rules; `brand-guidelines.md` → how we write |
| "Everything your club needs, all in one place" | Anything a generic booking company could also say | `brand-guidelines.md` → what we avoid |
| Never names the real trade | Say what the trade is. You get the booking, the marketplace keeps the customer. | `competitors.md` → white space |
| No number anywhere | €149 a month. Say the number. | `business-context.md` → differentiator 3 |
| "Start your free trial" | The path to a customer is a 20-minute call with Sofie. She does most of the calls. | `business-context.md` → GTM motion |

The headline in the second version works because it names what the club keeps and what it gives up in one line. The button works because it points at a real person, not a signup form. Neither came from the prompt. Both were already written down.

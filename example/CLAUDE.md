# Deuce marketing OS

<!-- Fictional company. Used to show what a filled context layer looks like. -->

## Who I am

- **Role:** Head of Marketing
- **Company:** Deuce (deuce.example)
- **Stage:** Seed, €1.8M raised January 2026

## What Deuce does

Booking, membership and court management for padel clubs. Your members book in your app, under your name, and you keep the relationship.

The alternative is a marketplace that handles your bookings and quietly becomes the brand your members are loyal to.

## Who we sell to

- **Primary buyer:** Owner of an independent padel club with 4 to 12 courts
- **Secondary:** The club manager, who lives in the booking system all day
- **Core pain:** Half the courts sit empty on Tuesday afternoon and are triple-booked on Thursday evening. No-shows cost roughly 11% of bookable revenue. The coaching schedule lives in a different spreadsheet entirely.

→ Full detail: `context/business-context.md`

## How we're positioned

Your club, your members, your data.

Playtomic fills your empty courts and takes the relationship with the player who booked it. We fill them and leave the member yours.

## Key differentiators

- **You own the member.** Your branding, your email list, your data. Export it any time.
- **Built by someone who ran a club.** Sofie ran a six-court club near Antwerp for four years. The dynamic pricing exists because Tuesday afternoons nearly killed her.
- **Flat fee, no commission.** €149 a month regardless of volume. Marketplaces take 8 to 15% of every booking they source.

## GTM motion

- **Motion:** Sales-led, founder-led at this stage
- **Path to customer:** club owner communities and word of mouth → website → 20-minute call with Sofie → 30-day trial → close
- **Sales cycle:** 2 to 5 weeks. Faster after a bad month.
- **Decision:** Owner decides, usually alone. Club manager can veto on usability.

## Competitors to track

- **Direct:** Playtomic, Matchi
- **Indirect / status quo:** WhatsApp, a phone, and a laminated grid on the wall

→ Full analysis: `context/competitors.md`

## Rules

- **Language:** British / EU English
- **Voice:** warm, direct, slightly amused → full rules in `context/brand-guidelines.md`
- **Lead with:** the club, not the software. We're on the owner's side of the table.
- **Never:** "revolutionise", "seamless", "elevate your club", "the future of padel". No stock photos of people high-fiving.
- **Output conventions:** sentence case headlines, no em dashes, one idea per paragraph

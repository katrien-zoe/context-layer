## Pre-publish checklist

Before anything ships, ask:
1. Would a smart, sceptical reader find this useful?
2. Does this help someone make a decision?
3. Could this be shorter and clearer?
4. Are we saying something real — or just safe?

*If the answer isn't clear, rewrite.*
